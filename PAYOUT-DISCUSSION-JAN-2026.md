# What the league actually decided about payouts — Discord, Jan 2026
Source: discord-general-export.json (export ends 2026-03-20). Nothing after that date is covered.

## DECIDED
- **Buy-in raised to $250.** Josh James proposed it Jan 3 after seeing "those measly payouts."
  Quick vote A/same B/$200 C/$250. Seven votes for C. Jason: "7 votes for $250. Good enough to
  make it official?" Red Kingdom: "Yes it's official."
- **Voting-threshold rule stated by Red Kingdom (NOT in the written bylaws):**
  "The vote to increase the buy in needs 8. The amount just needs a majority."
  Jeremy on the same day: "I thought it got changed to a simple majority but maybe not.
  I can't keep it all straight anymore."

## PROPOSED, NEVER VOTED
Jason posted two structures on Jan 5 and asked for feedback:
- **Option 1 — Fixed Dollar Payouts.** Lock exact amounts per prize, unchanged until voted.
- **Option 2 — Pool-Based (Percent) Payouts.** Split the pot by percentage so prizes scale
  automatically with the buy-in. Jason: "The percentage based does account for any additional
  buy-in amounts like insurance / luxury."

No vote on either option appears anywhere in the export. The thread died into Chiefs talk.

## THE INSURANCE QUESTION — open
Jeremy, twice: "Fold insurance for everyone into the higher buy in and then raise luxury tax.
In other words the higher buy in includes insurance." Ryan: "I like this." Jason agreed in
principle but wanted it kept separate and set by vote. Red Kingdom: "Luxury tax yes insurance
maybe" and then "Place the insurance tie in for a vote."
**That vote never happened.** Today insurance was charged separately at $15 to six teams.

## LUXURY TAX — direction agreed, number never set
Consensus that it should go UP with the buy-in. Jeremy: "the lux tax should be far more punitive
to make it a real decision" and noted "Phil paid the most in luxury tax last year and didn't even
make the playoffs." Chris Drake asked directly: "if we are increasing the buyin then shouldn't the
price of luxury tax and insurance go up too?"

## OPINIONS ON INDIVIDUAL PAYOUTS (no votes)
- Jeremy: weekly top score should NOT rise. "It almost always ends up carrying over and gets
  raised naturally... people don't need to be winning $150 on getting the high score some random
  week." Suggested dropping the outscore-by-10 rule to 5, or removing it.
- Chad Nichols: kill a wildcard payout, move that money to the league/division winners.
- Chris Drake: "everything other than finishing 1st, 2nd, 3rd, and division winner can eat butt"
  but "I do have a soft spot in my heart for unluckiest."
- Jeremy against trimming: "The leagues I've been in that just have payouts for 1st, 2nd, 3rd and
  TB aren't nearly as fun. Especially if you aren't a top 4-5 team."

## OTHER THINGS RAISED IN THE SAME THREAD, ALSO UNRESOLVED
- Josh Lennon: outlaw keeper BUMPING entirely (his other league did). If you want two 2nd-round
  keepers you must own two 2nd-round picks. Jeremy pushed back that it just shifts the advantage
  to trade-heavy owners rather than solving parity.
- Chad: move FA/waiver day to Tuesday. Jason seconded it for a vote. No vote recorded.
- Chad: replace fake blind-bid dollars with real money.
- Jason: everyone starts with set BB, then paid re-buy-ins add more.
