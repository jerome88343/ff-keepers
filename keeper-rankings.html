Straight talent. Who is walking into the draft with the best four players, ignoring what they paid to get them. Numbers beside each keeper are his overall expert consensus rank.
How it is scored
Each keeper earns points on a decay curve, so the #1 player is worth far more than the #10, who is worth far more than the #100.
Why not add up ranks
Flat rank-adding would rate four ordinary players above one superstar and three fillers. It does not work that way in a lineup.
