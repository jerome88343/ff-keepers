# Reading Chad's proposal — the interpretation we are building on

## The conclusion
The Player Tax is a PURE ADD-ON. Keeper pricing does not change at all.
"Original draft cost/ADP" is Chad's shorthand for the cost mechanism we already have
(drafted round the first keeper year, ADP/ECR every year after). The "three seasons" he
refers to are three TAX-FREE seasons, not three seasons at a frozen price.

## The reasoning

1. HIS OWN PURPOSE STATEMENT ARGUES AGAINST THE FROZEN-PRICE READING.
   He opens by naming the problem as "preventing elite players from being kept indefinitely
   at their original low draft cost." A rule that freezes players at their original low draft
   cost forever would CAUSE that problem, not prevent it. The tax is his prevention mechanism,
   layered on top of pricing that already escalates.

2. THE SLASH IS DOING THE WORK.
   He writes "draft cost/ADP" and "draft position/ADP" every single time, never one without
   the other. That is not a new flat price. That is him naming both halves of the existing
   rule in one breath: draft position if it is your first keeper year, ADP if it is not.

3. HIS 11:59 MESSAGE SPELLS OUT THE TIMELINE EXPLICITLY.
   "Year 1 drafted / Year 2 keep in draft spot/adp / Year 3 ADP / Year 4 pay"
   Year 3 at ADP is exactly today's rule. He is describing the current system with a fee
   bolted on at year four.

4. THE TABLE'S COST COLUMN IS STATIC FILLER, NOT THE PROPOSAL.
   Every row of his table reads "Original draft position" and never changes. The only column
   that moves is the tax. If he intended to change the cost basis, the cost column would be
   the interesting part of his own table. It is inert, because it is not what he is proposing.

5. TWELVE OWNERS READ IT AND NOBODY REACTED TO A GIVEAWAY.
   A permanent draft-round discount on every keeper would be the largest rule change in the
   league's history. Phil, Ryan, Jeremy, Josh and Lennon all replied and every single reply
   treated it purely as a money question. Nobody said "so I keep JSN at a 7th forever?"
   That silence is the strongest evidence of all for how the room read it.

## What "three seasons" means under this reading
Year 1 you draft him. Year 2 is the first keeper year. Year 3 is the second keeper year.
Those three are tax-free. The tax begins in year 4, the third keeper year. That maps exactly
onto his section headers.

## What would flip this
If Chad says he meant the cost to freeze at the original draft round, this reading is wrong
and the proposal is a far bigger change than the thread treated it as. He gets the final say.
