# Jason's two payout structures — posted to #general, Jan 5 2026, 8:45 PM
Verbatim. Never voted on as of the Mar 20 2026 export.

## Option 1 — Fixed Dollar Payouts

We lock in exact dollar amounts for each prize. Every season, payouts stay the same (unless we
vote to change them), and any leftover money (from team count differences) goes to a predefined
place (ex: 1st place or league fund).

Payouts (Fixed):
- 1st: $1,250
- 2nd: $625
- 3rd: $250
- Toilet Bowl: $125
- Division Winners: $75 each ($225 total)
- Survivor: $100
- Weekly Top Score (Weeks 1-14): $15/week ($210 total)
- Weekly Wild Card (Weeks 1-14): $15/week ($210 total)

Best for: simplicity, everyone knows the exact prizes up front.
Tradeoff: if the buy-in changes, the pot changes but prizes don't automatically scale.

## Option 2 — Pool-Based (Percent) Payouts

We split the total prize pot into "pools" by percentage, and prizes scale automatically if buy-in
changes. The structure stays the same, the dollars adjust based on the pot.

Pool Structure:
- Championship Pool: 70% of total pot -> pays 1st/2nd/3rd (3rd gets buy-in back; remainder split
  70/30 between 1st/2nd)
- Weekly Pool (Weeks 1-14): 15% of total pot -> split 50/50 between Weekly High Score + Wild Card
- Season Awards Pool: 15% of total pot -> Division Winners + Survivor + Toilet Bowl

Best for: fairness and consistency across different buy-in amounts; prizes scale automatically.
Tradeoff: you won't know the exact payout dollars until the buy-in total is final (though we can
estimate).
