Option 1 · Fixed Dollar

Every prize is a set number. Nobody has to do math and everybody knows what they are playing for on day one.

How the remainder works

At this year's pot the fixed prizes come to $2,995, leaving $241 for the league to put to use. The one thing to know is that it does not re-scale on its own — if the buy-in moves again, the prizes stay put and the remainder changes size.

Option 2 · Percent of Pot

Three pools: 70% championship, 15% weekly, 15% season awards. Distributes the whole pot every year and re-scales itself automatically the next time the buy-in moves.

The catch

Nobody knows exact dollars until every buy-in is in, and the numbers come out uneven. Under this, first place clears $1,410 and there is no remainder, so nothing is left for other uses.

One thing Option 2 never spelled out

The season awards pool is 15% of the pot, which is $485 this year, and it is supposed to cover three division winners, survivor and the toilet bowl. Jason's post never said how that $485 splits between those five prizes. If we go with Option 2, that split needs deciding before the season starts.
